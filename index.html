How it stops people marking for others

Layer 1

Live code, short-lived session

The code inside the QR expires in 30 seconds and the form must be submitted within 2 minutes of scanning. A forwarded link opens to "expired".

Stops remote proxies
Layer 2

One phone, one person, one day

After the first check-in, the ID is tied to that phone. A second ID from the same phone is blocked and flagged. A new phone on day 2 goes to a small help desk.

Stops one person doing many
Layer 3

Signed declaration and spot checks

Every record keeps the exact declaration wording, its version and a timestamp. Two or three staff verify a few attendees in the hall each session.
